How Rumbella helps your city achieve its sustainability goals

A tailored solution to help your municipality meet its sustainability objectives and foster economic and environmental resilience.

Data Driven Insights

Detailed data on waste reduction, emissions avoidance, and community impact, enabling your municipality to track progress toward EU and national targets.

Reduce Food Waste and CO₂ Emissions

Rumbella enables dining facilities, retailers, and producers to track, redistribute, and repurpose surplus food, reducing environmental impact and aligning with SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption) and SDG 13 (Climate Action).

Ehanced Food Security

Rumbella connects surplus food with donation partners, ensuring edible food reaches those in need, strengthening community ties, and supporting SDG 2 (Zero Hunger).

Advance a Circular Economy

Rumbella partners with local players to convert non-consumable food into compost, animal feed, or energy, fostering resource efficiency in line with SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption).

Generate Economic Value

Rumbella helps municipalities earn revenue through transaction commissions and vendor and partner fees. Businesses benefit by reducing disposal costs and generating income from surplus sales, aligning with SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth).

Engage Communities and Educate Future Generations

Rumbella collaborates with universities and community groups to offer hands-on learning experiences, promoting long-term sustainability practices in line with SDG 4 (Quality Education).
